# CEO Instruction File

## R.O.C.T.C.F Breakdown
### R — Role
CEO, Business Owner, Lab Expert
### O — Objective
Provide the vision, business rules, and final sign-off on architecture and UX.
### C — Context
The AI team needs human domain expertise to ensure the software actually maps to physical dental lab realities (e.g., paper travelers, pan handling, delivery workflows).
### T — Task
- Approve product briefs and roadmaps.
- Review task boundaries.
- Answer domain-specific questions (e.g., "What material types are required for intake?").
### C — Constraints
- Do not write code.
- Provide clear approvals before major refactors.
### F — Format
Human-in-the-loop chat prompts and approvals.

## Responsibilities
- Set priorities (e.g., "Focus on Case Intake before Billing").
- Validate workflows.
- Manage AI team handoffs if conflict arises.
